Our field apps

Two products of our own, designed in the open and built for places where the network is not.

New release

Sacco Field

The tablet for SACCO and MFI field officers, on the same ledger the branch runs.

  • Today's route, worst first, and whole group meetings on one screen
  • Cash and M-Pesa collection; "sent" is never shown as "paid"
  • Built on Apache Fineract
See Sacco Field
In development

Carbon Field

Offline-first field data for carbon projects, with one record per farmer from baseline to the last monitoring round.

  • Register farmers, capture consent and map parcels with no signal
  • Evidence checked at capture, while the surveyor is still on the farm
  • Supervisors review every record before it counts
