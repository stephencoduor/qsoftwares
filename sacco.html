Sacco Field, in the field

A tablet app for the field officer, built for a market with no signal. Everything is saved on the tablet first and sent when it can be.

  • Today's route, worst first, from the collection sheet
  • Group meetings with attendance and a keypad per member
  • Cash, M-Pesa push, paybill and passbook deposits
  • The member's file: accounts, guarantors, collateral, next of kin

What the officer collects at 10:15 goes to the branch ledger when the tablet next has a signal — and nothing on the tablet says Posted before the ledger has confirmed it.

A day with Sacco Field

Follow one field officer from the branch to the market and back.

Every screen below is the app itself on a tablet, running its labelled demo dataset: Alice Wanjiru's Kiambu route and the Umoja Self-Help Group. The names and amounts are that sample data.

07:40
The route

The day opens worst first

Fourteen stops from the collection sheet, split into overdue, upcoming and paid. The member twelve days behind is at the top, not the one nearest the branch. Tap a stop and it opens beside the list, so the rest of the day never leaves the screen.

08:30
The group meeting

Fourteen members, one screen, a keypad each

Umoja meets at Gikomba. Each row shows what the member still owes today and their outstanding balance; a long-press marks them present, absent or late. Tap Collect repayment and a pane opens with its own keypad, and the send button sits just above the keys.

A member who has paid half today owes the rest, not the whole — so the row says the rest.

10:15
A stall, in cash

Three steps, then a receipt

Evidence, amount and channel, confirm. The officer carries the cash; the tablet records who paid, how much, where and when. Payment Successful appears only once the ledger has taken it and the tablet has read it back — and the receipt can be shared from the same dialog.

11:00
An M-Pesa push

"Sent" is not "paid"

An STK push is a request the member has to accept on their own phone. So the tablet says PUSH SENT! and Awaiting client confirmation, and the member's row stays Waiting to sync until M-Pesa answers. Open that loan again from anywhere — the route, the picker, the loan page — and the tablet shows the prompt already on the phone instead of inviting a second one.

12:30
A member's file

Everything about a member, in one place

Client info, accounts, identification, charges, guarantors, next of kin, collateral and a pinpoint of where the business is. From a loan, Pay full amount opens the collect pane on the loan's total outstanding, with the member's M-Pesa number already filled in.

14:00
A deposit

Into the passbook, at the meeting

From the meeting's + button: Deposit, then the member. The pane names the savings account and its balance, and the new balance is read back from the ledger after the deposit posts. An account the branch has not activated yet says so, and takes no amount.

Money out is always a withdrawal request the branch approves. The tablet never pays out.

16:45
Back in signal

Saved first, sent when it can be

The Sync centre lists everything the tablet holds: what failed first, with the server's own reason, then what is waiting, then what is done, kept for thirty days. Sync runs by itself every fifteen minutes and whenever the connection returns; Sync now sends it straight away.

The rule the whole app keeps

Four words. Never a fifth.

A field app that says paid when it means sent teaches officers to tell members something the ledger will contradict tomorrow. Sacco Field has four words for where money is, and uses no others.

Waiting to sync Posted Failed Discarded
Saved · Waiting to sync

On the tablet, with a reference, and plainly not yet in the ledger.

Push sent

A request on the member's phone. Nothing is collected until they accept it.

Payment Successful

Only after the server has taken it and the tablet has read it back.

Failed

The server's own words. Edit & retry, or Discard by typing the amount back.

When the connection drops mid-send and the server will not say whether the money arrived, the tablet does not guess: it names the reference to look for on the account and asks the branch before anything is sent again. Rendered from the app's screenshot tests.

Under the hood

A native app, and the core it posts to.

The tablet app

Native Android in Kotlin and Jetpack Compose, with an on-device database that every capture is written to first.

The outbox

Every capture carries one idempotency key, sent three ways. A retry after a dropped connection is checked against the ledger before anything is sent again.

Apache Fineract

The ledger, with the Sacco custom modules for mobile money and the collection sheet. The tablet reads back what it posted before it says so.

Payment Hub

M-Pesa and Airtel collection for the branch. The tablet's STK push goes through the Sacco mobile-money module on the same core.
